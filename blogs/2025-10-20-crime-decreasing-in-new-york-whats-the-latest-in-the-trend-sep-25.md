Incidents decreased to 37,904 for Sep-2025 up from 38,349 for Aug-2025 (-1.2% change). Among the types of offenses that saw an uptick last month, reports of Burglary/Breaking & Entering went from 2,029 to 2,221 incidents (+9.5% change).
