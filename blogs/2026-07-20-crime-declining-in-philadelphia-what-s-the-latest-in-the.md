The number of crime incidents in Philadelphia saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 14,035 for June-2026 down from 14,468 for May-2026 (-3% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Drug/Narcotic Violations and Simple Assault.
