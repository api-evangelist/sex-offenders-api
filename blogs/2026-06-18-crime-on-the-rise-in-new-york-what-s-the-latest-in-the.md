The number of crime incidents in New York saw an overall increase last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents increased to 33,799 for May-2026 up from 32,164 for Apr-2026 (+5.1% change). Among the types of offenses that saw an uptick last month, reports of Aggravated Assault of went from 567 to 626 incidents (+10.4% change).
