The number of crime incidents in Minneapolis saw an overall increase last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents increased to 4,326 for Jul-2026 up from 3,862 for Jun-2026 (+12.0% change). Among the types of offenses that saw an uptick last month, reports of Destruction/Damage/Vandalism of Property went from 562 to 867 incidents (+54.3% change).
