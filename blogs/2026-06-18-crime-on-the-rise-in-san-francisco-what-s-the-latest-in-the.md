The number of crime incidents in San Francisco saw an overall increase last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents increased to 7,614 for May-2026 up from 7,094 for Apr-2026 (+7.3% change). Among the types of offenses that saw an uptick last month, reports of Simple Assault went from 343 to 446 incidents (+30% change).
