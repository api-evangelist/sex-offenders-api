The number of crime incidents in Denver saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 5,036 for Nov-2025 down from 5,440 for Oct-2025 (-7.4% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Simple Assault and Burglary/Breaking & Entering.
