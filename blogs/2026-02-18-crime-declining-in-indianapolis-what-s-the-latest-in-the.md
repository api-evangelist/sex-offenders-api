The number of crime incidents in Indianapolis saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 28,127 for Jan-2026 down from 28,883 for Dec-2025 (-2.6% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Robbery and Theft From Motor Vehicle.
