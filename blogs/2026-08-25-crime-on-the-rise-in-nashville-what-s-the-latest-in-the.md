The number of crime incidents in Nashville saw an overall increase last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents increased to 8,707 for Jul-2026 up from 8,558 for Jun-2026 (+1.7% change). Among the types of offenses that saw an uptick last month, reports of Aggravated Assault went from 832 to 876 incidents (+5.3% change).
