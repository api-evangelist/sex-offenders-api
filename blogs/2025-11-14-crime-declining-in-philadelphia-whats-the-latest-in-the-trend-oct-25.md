The number of crime incidents in Philadelphia saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 13,386 for Oct-2025 down from 14,300 for Sep-2025 (-6.4% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Destruction/Damage/Vandalism of Property and Burglary/Breaking & Entering.
