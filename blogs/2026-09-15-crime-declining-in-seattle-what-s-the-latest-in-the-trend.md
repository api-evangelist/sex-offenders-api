The number of crime incidents in Seattle saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous decrease, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 4,455 for Aug-2026 down from 4,792 for Jul-2026 (-7% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Drug/Narcotic Violations and Destruction/Damage/Vandalism of Property.
