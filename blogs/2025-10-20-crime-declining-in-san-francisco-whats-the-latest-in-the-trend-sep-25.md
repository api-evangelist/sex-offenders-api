The number of crime incidents in San Francisco saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 6,906 for Sep-2025 down from 7,392 for Aug-2025 (-6.6% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Theft From Motor Vehicle and Shoplifting.
