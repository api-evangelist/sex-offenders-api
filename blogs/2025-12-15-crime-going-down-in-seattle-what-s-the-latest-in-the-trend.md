The number of crime incidents in Seattle saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 4,706 for Nov-2025 down from 5,478 for Oct-2025 (-14.1% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Motor Vehicle Theft and Theft From Motor Vehicle.
