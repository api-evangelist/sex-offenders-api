The number of crime incidents in Denver saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 4,596 for Feb-2026 down from 5,127 for Jan-2026 (-10.4% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Robbery and Theft From Motor Vehicle.
