The number of crime incidents in Denver saw an overall decrease last month, after a previous rise, according to data from CrimeoMeter. Incidents fell to 5,449 for Aug-2026 down from 5,688 for Jul-2026 (-4.2% change). The offenses that decreased the most were Robbery and Motor Vehicle Theft.
